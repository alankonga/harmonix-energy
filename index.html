COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request a quotation.

How do I know what size of solar system I need?

It depends on your electricity use, the appliances you want to power and whether you need backup during outages. Try our calculator above for a rough idea, or send us your monthly bill or a list of appliances and we will recommend a suitable size.

Will my system work during a power outage?

That depends on the design. A system with batteries and a hybrid inverter can supply power during an outage. A basic grid-tied system without storage normally shuts down for safety when the grid fails. We will explain which option fits your needs.

How long does an installation take?

The time depends on the size and complexity of the system and the site. We give you an estimated schedule in your quotation after understanding your project.

What warranty and support do I get?

Equipment is covered by the manufacturer's warranty, and we state the terms for your system in the quotation. We also offer inspection and maintenance to help keep your system safe and performing well.

Which areas do you serve?

Contact us with your location and we will confirm whether we can serve your site.
